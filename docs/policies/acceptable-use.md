# Acceptable use policy

**Status: draft, not in force.** Written 2026-11-11 for review. It has not been read by a lawyer. Until it is published on the website, the terms of service govern.

This policy says what you may send through i10, what you may not, and what we do when the rules are broken. It applies to everything sent or received through i10: the email API, broadcasts, templates, hosted mailboxes, and anything a customer of yours sends through your account.

Every rule has a short code, such as `AUP-C1`. When we limit, hold or close an account, we tell you which rule the decision rests on. If we cannot name a rule, we do not act.

## 1. The short version

- Send only to people who asked for your mail or who expect it because of something they did with you.
- Say honestly who you are and what the message is.
- Never send phishing, malware, scams or illegal content.
- Make leaving easy, and honor it.
- Keep your bounces and complaints low.
- Do not try to get around a limit, a hold or a closed account.

## 2. Consent and recipients

| Code     | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `AUP-R1` | **Transactional mail needs a reason.** You may send a message that a person triggered or needs: a receipt, a password reset, a security alert, a shipping notice, an invitation they were told to expect.                   |
| `AUP-R2` | **Marketing mail needs consent.** Newsletters, promotions and announcements go only to people who asked for them, clearly and for that sender. A pre-ticked box, a line buried in terms, or a business card is not consent. |
| `AUP-R3` | **No bought, rented, traded or scraped lists.** This includes addresses harvested from websites, public directories, social networks, data brokers or another company's customer list.                                      |
| `AUP-R4` | **No cold outreach.** Unsolicited sales or prospecting email to people you have no relationship with is not allowed, whether sent to one address or a million, and whether the recipient is a person or a business.         |
| `AUP-R5` | **No guessing or testing addresses.** Do not send mail to find out which addresses exist, and do not send to addresses built from patterns.                                                                                 |
| `AUP-R6` | **Keep proof.** For marketing mail, keep a record of when and how each person consented. We may ask for it during a review, and "we cannot show it" is treated as "there was none".                                         |
| `AUP-R7` | **Consent goes stale.** Do not start mailing a list you have not written to in over twelve months without first confirming those people still want it.                                                                      |

## 3. Unsubscribes and suppression

| Code     | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `AUP-U1` | **Every marketing message can be left in one step.** It carries a working unsubscribe link, and i10 adds one-click unsubscribe headers to broadcasts. Do not remove or hide them.                                                      |
| `AUP-U2` | **Honor it within two days,** and sooner where the law says so. No login, no survey, no fee, no "are you sure" maze.                                                                                                                   |
| `AUP-U3` | **Do not mail suppressed addresses.** An address that hard-bounced, complained or unsubscribed stays off your list. Do not re-import it, and do not work around suppression by changing the sending domain, the workspace or the list. |
| `AUP-U4` | **Transactional mail is not a loophole.** Do not put promotions in a receipt or a password reset to reach people who unsubscribed from marketing.                                                                                      |

## 4. Honesty about who you are

| Code     | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `AUP-H1` | **Send only from domains you own or control.** You verify each domain with us before sending.                                                                                                                                        |
| `AUP-H2` | **No impersonation.** Do not pretend to be another company, brand, government body or person, in the sender name, the address, the design, a logo or the wording.                                                                    |
| `AUP-H3` | **No misleading headers or subjects.** The `From`, `Reply-To` and subject line must not deceive the reader about who sent the message or what it is. No fake "Re:" or "Fwd:", no false urgency about an account that does not exist. |
| `AUP-H4` | **Links go where they say.** Link text that shows one address must not lead to another. Do not hide the destination behind chains of redirects.                                                                                      |
| `AUP-H5` | **Identify yourself where the law requires it.** Marketing mail includes your real business name and a valid postal address where laws such as CAN-SPAM, GDPR or CASL apply to you.                                                  |
| `AUP-H6` | **Tell us the truth.** The information you give us about your business, your mail and your identity must be accurate, at signup and during any review.                                                                               |

## 5. Content that is never allowed

These are closed immediately when confirmed. There is no warning step.

| Code     | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `AUP-C1` | **Phishing and credential theft.** Any message that tries to obtain passwords, codes, payment details, recovery phrases or personal data under false pretences, or that links to a page that does.                                                 |
| `AUP-C2` | **Malware.** Any attachment or link that delivers a virus, ransomware, spyware, a remote access tool or any code meant to harm or control a device without consent. This includes scripts in HTML attachments and files disguised as another type. |
| `AUP-C3` | **Fraud and scams.** Advance-fee schemes, fake invoices and fake overdue notices, fake prizes, fake job offers, fake charities, romance scams, tech-support scams, and business email compromise.                                                  |
| `AUP-C4` | **Child sexual abuse material and exploitation.** We report it to the authorities as the law requires.                                                                                                                                             |
| `AUP-C5` | **Threats, harassment and incitement.** Threats of violence, targeted harassment, doxxing, and content that incites violence or hatred against people for who they are.                                                                            |
| `AUP-C6` | **Sexual content shared without consent,** including fabricated images of real people.                                                                                                                                                             |
| `AUP-C7` | **Illegal goods and services.** Illegal drugs, weapons sold unlawfully, stolen data or accounts, forged documents, and anything unlawful where you or your recipients are.                                                                         |
| `AUP-C8` | **Infringement and counterfeits.** Pirated content, counterfeit goods, and use of another party's trademarks or copyrighted work without the right to do so.                                                                                       |
| `AUP-C9` | **Deceptive money and health claims.** "Guaranteed returns", get-rich-quick programmes, pump-and-dump promotions, miracle cures and fake medical claims.                                                                                           |

You may send them only where it is legal for you and every recipient, with explicit consent, and after we have approved your use in writing. Without approval they are treated as a violation.

| Code     | Category                                                     |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `AUP-A1` | Gambling, betting, lotteries and sweepstakes                 |
| `AUP-A2` | Adult content and services                                   |
| `AUP-A3` | Alcohol, tobacco, vaping and cannabis                        |
| `AUP-A4` | Lending, credit repair, debt collection and debt relief      |
| `AUP-A5` | Investment products, cryptocurrency and trading signals      |
| `AUP-A6` | Prescription medicines, supplements and weight-loss products |
| `AUP-A7` | Political campaigning and fundraising                        |
| `AUP-A8` | Affiliate and multi-level marketing                          |

To ask for approval, write to compliance@i10.tech with what you send, to whom, how they consented, and the licences you hold.

## 7. How you send

| Code     | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `AUP-S1` | **Keep hard bounces low.** Stay below 4% of the mail you send. At 10% and above we limit or stop your sending. A high bounce rate means the list is old, bought or guessed.                                                         |
| `AUP-S2` | **Keep complaints low.** Stay below 0.1%, one complaint in a thousand messages. At 0.3% and above we limit or stop your sending. These are the same numbers Gmail and Yahoo apply to you.                                           |
| `AUP-S3` | **We do not judge rates on a handful of messages.** Below about a hundred messages in the period we look at counts, not percentages. One bounce in three messages is not a 33% bounce rate.                                         |
| `AUP-S4` | **Do not spread out to hide.** Do not split sending across workspaces, accounts, domains or API keys to stay under a limit or to dilute a bad reputation.                                                                           |
| `AUP-S5` | **One closed account means closed.** If we suspend or close an account for abuse, do not open another, yourself or through someone else. We keep a record of confirmed abuse after an account is deleted, for exactly this purpose. |
| `AUP-S6` | **Do not manipulate reputation.** No inbox warm-up networks, no paid engagement, no mail sent only to make a domain or address look trusted, no seed-list gaming.                                                                   |
| `AUP-S7` | **Grow at a human pace.** A new sender ramps up over days and weeks. A sudden jump to large volume from a new account is held for review.                                                                                           |
| `AUP-S8` | **Attachments.** Do not send executable files or scripts, files whose real type differs from their name, password-protected archives meant to avoid scanning, or documents with macros. We refuse messages that carry them.         |

## 8. Sending for your own customers

| Code     | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `AUP-P1` | **You may build on i10.** If your product sends mail for your own customers, that is allowed.                                                                                                                                      |
| `AUP-P2` | **You answer for them.** This policy applies to their mail as it does to yours. You must have terms that bind them to these rules, a way to stop a customer who breaks them, and a contact who answers us within one business day. |
| `AUP-P3` | **Do not resell raw access.** Do not hand out i10 API keys or SMTP access to third parties, and do not offer i10 as an open relay.                                                                                                 |

## 9. Security and fair use of the platform

| Code     | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `AUP-X1` | **Protect your keys.** Keep API keys and passwords secret, scope them, and rotate them if they may have leaked. Tell us at once if you think an account is compromised. You are responsible for mail sent with your keys until you do. |
| `AUP-X2` | **Do not attack or probe.** No scanning, load testing or attempts to bypass limits, authentication or isolation on i10 or through it, without our written agreement.                                                                   |
| `AUP-X3` | **Do not interfere with other customers.** This includes using shared sending reputation carelessly. One sender's abuse harms delivery for everyone.                                                                                   |
| `AUP-X4` | **Mailboxes are for correspondence.** Hosted mailboxes are for people writing to people. Do not use them for bulk sending, as a drop for fraud proceeds or stolen credentials, or to register accounts in bulk elsewhere.              |
| `AUP-X5` | **Do not evade our checks.** Do not obfuscate text, hide content in images or attachments to avoid scanning, cloak links, or vary content to defeat duplicate detection.                                                               |

## 10. How we check

- **Automated checks run on everything.** We look at bounce and complaint rates, sending patterns, the sites your messages link to, the files you attach, and the content of messages. Automated systems, including machine-learning models, read message content for this purpose.
- **Signals are evidence, not verdicts.** An automated check can slow or pause sending. It cannot close an account.
- **People decide the serious cases.** A person reviews every hold within 24 hours and every suspension before it becomes permanent.
- **A decision made by a person stays made.** Once a reviewer has cleared a concern, we do not raise the same concern again without new evidence.

## 11. What happens when a rule is broken

We choose the mildest step that stops the harm.

| Step           | What it means                                                                                                                      |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Notice**     | We tell you what we saw and which rule it concerns. Nothing is restricted.                                                         |
| **Limits**     | Your sending volume is reduced while the problem is fixed.                                                                         |
| **Hold**       | New messages are accepted and kept, not delivered, until a person has reviewed the account. Nothing is lost if the hold is lifted. |
| **Suspension** | Sending stops. You keep access to your data and can appeal.                                                                        |
| **Closure**    | The account is closed and the abuse is recorded under `AUP-S5`.                                                                    |

We skip straight to suspension or closure for anything in section 5, for malware or phishing links, for evading a previous decision, and when the law or our providers require it. We may also act without notice when waiting would cause harm to recipients or to other customers' delivery.

When we act, we tell you the step, the rule code and what you can do next. We do not describe the specific signals we used, because that would teach abusers to avoid them.

## 12. Appeals

- **Who:** anyone whose account was limited, held, suspended or closed.
- **How:** reply to the notice or write to appeals@i10.tech from the account owner's address.
- **What to include:** what you send and to whom, how those people came to be on your list, what went wrong, and what you have changed. Specifics decide appeals. "Please reinstate my account" does not.
- **How long:** we answer within two business days. A hold is reviewed within 24 hours whether or not you appeal.
- **Who decides:** a person. An appeal is never refused by an automated system alone.
- **Once is not the limit:** you may appeal again with new information. Repeating the same appeal does not change the answer.

## 13. Reporting abuse

If you received unwanted or harmful mail sent through i10, forward it with full headers to abuse@i10.tech. We read every report and act on confirmed abuse. We do not share the reporter's identity with the sender.

## 14. Changes

We may change this policy as abuse and the law change. For changes that restrict what you may send, we give at least 30 days' notice by email, unless a shorter time is needed to stop harm or comply with the law. The date at the top shows the current version.

## Notes for i10 staff (not part of the published policy)

- **Codes are stable.** `AUP-R1` and the rest are keys the risk engine and the review tools cite. Never renumber or reuse one. Retire a code and add a new one.
- **Numbers that must match the engine:** `AUP-S1` and `AUP-S2` describe `bounce.hard.7d` and `complaint.7d` in `apps/api/src/risk/rules.ts`, and `AUP-S3` describes their minimum-volume guards. Change them together.
- **Decisions taken in this draft that a human should confirm:**
  1. Cold outreach is banned outright (`AUP-R4`). Resend and Postmark take the same line. Allowing it is the single largest source of complaints for a sending platform.
  2. Section 6 categories need written approval and are not simply banned.
  3. The twelve-month stale-consent rule (`AUP-R7`).
  4. The 24-hour hold review and two-business-day appeal answer are promises that need staff cover.
  5. The mailboxes `compliance@`, `appeals@` and `abuse@` must exist before publishing.
- **Not covered here and needed elsewhere:** the privacy policy must say that message content is analysed by automated systems, name the processors that do it, and state that a record of confirmed abuse is kept after account deletion.
- **Legal review needed** before publishing, in particular sections 10 to 12 (automated decision-making under GDPR Article 22) and `AUP-C4` (reporting duties).
