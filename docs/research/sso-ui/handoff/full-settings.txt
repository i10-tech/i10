<project>
i10, a bun monorepo. You work in your own git worktree on the console app, apps/console (Next.js; read apps/console/AGENTS.md). Four other agents work at the same time on other areas in other worktrees.

The owner cares intensely about micro-interactions and catches every jerk by eye. Your job is that they stop having to. These are their words about what is wrong:
- Something that appears out of thin air, a split second after the rest, is not acceptable. A step or panel must animate open with its content already inside. Reference: Profile & security, Security tab, adding another email (components/account/): it animates down with the input already there.
- A list that goes from 1 item to 0 must animate its last item and its container away. No jump at the end of an animation (e.g. a flex gap that stays until an element unmounts).
- Parts of a page must load together. If one card is there at once and another pops in later, the slow one's data belongs in the page's server render (fetch it in page.tsx, pass it as a prop) so they arrive together. Never let late content move the page: reserve the space or render it with the page.
- Errors under an input go in the input's own reserved hint area (FloatingInput `hint`, `state="invalid"`, reserveHint) so nothing below moves; messages needing more room roll open smoothly, using what the field already does. Say exactly what is wrong, never a generic error.
- Anything pressed gives feedback at once: a button that navigates or waits shows it was pressed (spinner or pending state), with no width change.
- Do not choose for the user: never auto-open or auto-select something they did not ask for.
- Do not start something the user has not finished yet (no checking a DNS record we only just displayed).
Learn the principle from those examples and hunt for the same class of problem everywhere in your area, whether or not it is listed.
</project>

Add --frames to save 30 fps screenshots to .motion/ and open them to look. See flows/sso.json for the format. The first run of a route compiles and can drop frames, so warm a page with curl first, and re-run before trusting a finding. Timing noise exists, so judge findings, and do not chase a one-off that fails to repeat. Write your flow file covering every interaction on your pages: loads, opening and closing things, adding and removing rows, errors, tabs, dialogs. Run it BEFORE changing anything and keep the output. Do not edit sampler.js or check.ts: if the tool is wrong about something, say so in the report.
</tool>
